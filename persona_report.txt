--- Note on Methodology ---
As documented in README.md, labeled real-user persona datasets are unavailable.
A 3,000-sample dataset was constructed with 6 preference features (1-5 scale)
and labels derived via domain-engineered heuristic scoring with a 4% random noise mask.
